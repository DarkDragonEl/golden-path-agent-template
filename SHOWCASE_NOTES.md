of three buckets — a tracked issue, an `Annex_A_Open_Items_EN.md`

first sharing moment (`docs/phase-e-kickoff-plan.md` §6: "created once

## Entry format

Each entry: date, sharing moment it came from, one-line summary of the
feedback, and its triage disposition.

```

- **Feedback:** <what the colleague said/asked/noticed>
- **Disposition:** tracked issue (<link>) | Annex A observation (<OI-##>) | phase-two parking
- **Notes:** <anything that needs remembering>
```
