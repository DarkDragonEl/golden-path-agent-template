# Known Error: Namespace Quota Exhaustion After Bulk Deploy

**Symptom:** A namespace unexpectedly hits its resource quota shortly
after a bulk deployment (many workloads deployed in a short window),
blocking further deployments even though the team's steady-state usage is
well under quota.

**Root cause:** Bulk deploys can transiently over-request resources
during rolling updates (old and new replicas briefly coexisting), pushing
the namespace over quota even though steady-state usage afterward would
be fine.

**Workaround:** A documented workaround exists: stagger bulk deploys into
smaller batches, or request a temporary quota increase for the deploy
window via the Namespace Request and Quota Policy's out-of-cycle review
path. Tracked in the ITSM known-error record for Namespace Governance
Owner visibility.
