# Service Catalog Publishing Standard

This standard governs what a team must do before publishing a new entry
to the internal service catalog.

Before publishing, the entry must be reviewed by the Service Catalog
Curator. The entry must follow the standard's required metadata fields —
at minimum a name, an owning team, a description, and a link to the
underlying template or add-on it provisions. An entry missing any
required metadata field is not published, and the Curator returns it to
the submitting team for correction.

Once published, ongoing changes to an entry follow the Service Catalog
Entry Update Procedure.
