# Known Error: Ingress Certificate Renewal Race Condition

**Symptom:** An ingress certificate occasionally fails to renew even
though the renewal automation reports success, leaving the old
certificate serving until it expires.

**Root cause:** A race condition between the renewal automation writing
the new certificate and the ingress controller reloading it can cause the
controller to briefly reload the old certificate reference after the new
one was already issued.

**Workaround:** A documented workaround exists for the ingress
certificate renewal race condition: manually trigger an ingress
controller reload after a renewal that reports success but doesn't
appear to take effect within a few minutes. This is tracked for a
permanent fix as part of the certificate-management automation.
