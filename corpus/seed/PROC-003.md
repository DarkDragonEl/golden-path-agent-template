# Service Catalog Entry Update Procedure

This procedure describes how an already-published service catalog entry
is updated.

A team that owns a published entry submits a change describing what's
being updated (metadata, underlying template, or both) to the Service
Catalog Curator. The Curator reviews the change against the Service
Catalog Publishing Standard's required metadata fields before merging it.
Once merged, the updated entry is live immediately; there is no separate
staged-rollout step for catalog entry metadata changes.

If the underlying template or add-on itself changes in a way that affects
already-provisioned instances, the owning team is responsible for
communicating that separately — this procedure covers the catalog entry
itself, not migration of existing instances.
