# Known Error: Service Catalog Entry Sync Delay

**Symptom:** A newly published service catalog entry doesn't appear in
search results or browse listings right away, even though the Service
Catalog Curator confirmed it was published successfully.

**Root cause:** A known sync delay can cause a newly published service
catalog entry to not appear immediately — the search index that backs
catalog browsing refreshes on its own periodic cycle rather than
instantly on publish.

**Workaround:** No action needed in most cases; the entry becomes visible
once the next sync cycle completes. If an entry is still missing well
beyond that, contact the Service Catalog Curator to confirm the
publish actually completed rather than assuming a sync delay.
